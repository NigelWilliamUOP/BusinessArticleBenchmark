# Counterfactual variant design

## Purpose

The variants test transfer, not factual recall. Each changes at least two substantive dimensions of the paired anchor while keeping the core research-production capability and validator family stable.

| Pair | Capability held constant | Main changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| COMP-01 | Formal supply-chain optimisation | Demand interaction becomes asymmetric; two coupled constraints replace one capacity constraint. |
| COMP-02 | Dynamic disruption simulation | Panama drought replaces Suez blockage; time-varying transit restrictions and environmental/service outcomes are added. |
| COMP-03 | Interpretable cross-country ML | Digital government replaces innovation; governance outcome, period and missing-data structure change. |
| COMP-04 | Hybrid time-series forecasting | Economic policy uncertainty replaces VIX; lower-frequency data and exogenous predictors change the leakage problem. |
| SYN-01 | Systematic review and meta-analysis | Corporate boards replace political representation; environmental misconduct replaces corruption. |
| SYN-02 | Conceptual theory development | Organisational resilience and system resilience replace greenwashing and sustainability impact. |
| SYN-03 | Reproducible systematic review | Mid-career women in research-intensive organisations replace women at work generally. |
| SYN-04 | Reproducible systematic review | Healthcare replaces education; patient-safety mechanisms are added. |
| DATA-01 | Corporate-report panel and mediation | FTSE SmallCap, climate expertise and cost of debt replace Jordanian services, board traits and cost of equity. |
| DATA-02 | Panel construction and spatial analysis | EU member states replace Chinese provinces; digitalisation and transport-emissions productivity change the indicators. |
| DATA-03 | Input-output extraction and network ranking | UK and 2019 replace Malaysia and 2020; indirect exposure and concentration stress tests are added. |
| DATA-04 | Corporate-report content analysis | UK water utilities replace large Swedish firms; biodiversity claims are triangulated with regulator evidence. |

## Design constraints

1. The variant cannot be solved by replacing names in the anchor manuscript.
2. The variant must remain feasible from public digital inputs within the benchmark budget.
3. The main result cannot be specified in the public prompt.
4. Hidden validators should test reconstruction and internal validity, not conformity to one expected sign.
5. Source availability must be checked and frozen before an official scored run.

## Interpretation

A model that passes an anchor but fails its variant may have brittle task-specific competence or may rely on contaminated solution patterns. A model that passes both provides stronger evidence of transferable research capability. A model that fails the anchor but passes the variant should be inspected rather than treated automatically as inconsistent: some variants may be empirically easier even when structurally distinct.
